How the demo works

  1. Choose a tagged item: keys, jacket, backpack or remote.
  2. Turn your phone: the arrow always points toward the tag, like a compass.
  3. Walk toward it: the distance counts down in real time.
  4. Ring it when you’re close: the tag beeps so you can spot it.

In the real app, the position comes from Bluetooth signals between your phone and the tag.
